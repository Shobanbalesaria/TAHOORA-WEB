About Us

Established in 1995, Tahoora Soft Drinks – a unit of Janta Bottling – has been delivering refreshing beverages with a commitment to purity, quality, and authentic taste.
Located in Navapur, Maharashtra, our facility blends local tradition with modern bottling technology to produce soft drinks that are both hygienic and flavorful.

We specialize in 200ml PET bottles and 250ml glass bottles, all chilled to 0°C and filled using pure filtered water to ensure unmatched freshness.

Our delicious flavors include:
🍊 Orange Flavor – A burst of citrusy freshness
🌿 Zeera Masala – A traditional Indian soda infused with jeera (cumin)
🍋 Lime-Up – A tangy and zesty lemon-lime blend
🟡 Lemon Flavor – A crisp and sharp lemon soda
🍓 Rediance – A bold red berry soda with a vibrant taste
💥 Big Ball – A strong and fizzy drink for a power-packed refreshment
🔵 Blue Lagoon – A cool, blueberry-inspired soda with a refreshing twist
🌟 Exclusive Limbu Soda – Made with jeera (cumin) powder, available in a 300ml PET bottle for just ₹15

Backed by modern processes and a dedication to cleanliness, Tahoora proudly delivers "The Taste of Freshness."

Whether you're at a celebration, relaxing at home, or grabbing a quick refreshment from a local shop, Tahoora Soft Drinks are your ideal thirst-quencher.
