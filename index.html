About US

Since 1995, Tahoora Soft Drinks – a unit of Janta Bottling – has been serving refreshing beverages with a promise of purity and taste.
Located in Navapur, Maharashtra, our facility is rooted in local tradition and dedicated to delivering top-quality soft drinks that keep customers coming back for more.

We specialize in both 200ml PET bottles and 250ml glass bottles, chilled to 0-degree temperature and filled using pure filtered water.

Our delicious flavors include:
🍊 Orange Flavor – A burst of citrusy freshness
🌿 Zeera Masala – Classic Indian soda with jeera & spice
🍋 Lime Up – Tangy and zesty lemon-lime blend
🟡 Lemon Flavor – Crisp and sharp lemon taste
🌟 Exclusive Limbu Soda – Made with Jeera powder, available in 300ml PET bottle at ₹15

With a heritage that combines modern technology and a commitment to hygiene, Tahoora proudly represents "The Taste of Freshness."

Whether in a party, at home, or in a street-side shop, Tahoora Soda is your go-to refreshment.
